Medication preparation, Annex 81 proposal

  1. During regular home care for a patient with dementia, a nurse observes difficulty cooperating with treatment and confusion about the treatment.
  2. The nurse creates an Annex 81 proposal for weekly medication preparation, recording both nursing diagnoses and dementia as the underlying medical problem. The validity end date is no later than one year after the validity start date.
  3. The nurse assigns the proposal to a general practitioner. The practitioner accepts or rejects it and may add a comment. In the Word scenario, acceptance converts the proposal into a prescription, while rejection leaves it as a proposal. The decision is returned to the nurse, who prepares the medication and fills the pillbox weekly after acceptance.
