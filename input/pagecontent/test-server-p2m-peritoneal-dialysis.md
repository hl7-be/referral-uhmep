### Scenario

<ol>
<li>A patient with kidney failure and loss of blood pressure is treated at a convention centre and cannot change dialysis bags independently.</li>
<li>The centre's physician prescribes home nursing support three times a day for one year, up to 1095 sessions, with feedback required. The remarks specify a dialysis-fluid dwell time of two hours. The medical reason notes that support will only be needed as required once the patient learns to change the bags independently.</li>
</ol>

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.html).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
