### Scenario

<ol>
<li>A patient who is not enrolled in a diabetes care path has difficulty managing medication, blood glucose monitoring and daily diabetes routines.</li>
<li>The physician prescribes ten individual self-care education sessions of 30 minutes, once a month, with a maximum of ten sessions and no required feedback. The general remarks specify "10 sessions of 30 min".</li>
</ol>

For M2M, the organization submits creation on behalf of its internal professional.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.html).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.

#### Business Values

| Business Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Prescription / proposal type | Diabetes education without care trajectory |
| Requested care | Diabetes education without care trajectory |
| Patient | Patient pseudonym: {{SEC1}} / {{transitInfo}} |
| Requester | Physician, SSIN {{prescriberSsin}}, representing organization NIHII {{organizationNihii}} |
| Validity start | {{validityStartDate}} |
| Feedback required | No |
| Frequency | 1 time(s) per 1 month |
| Maximum sessions | 10 |
| Session duration | 30 minutes |
| Education type | Education on diabetes self-care |
| Session type | Individual |
| Diagnosis / medical problem, before encryption | Difficulty with diabetes self-management |
| General remarks, before encryption | 10 sessions of 30 min |
| Creation date | {{createdAt}} |
