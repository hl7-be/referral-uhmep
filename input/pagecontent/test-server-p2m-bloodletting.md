### Scenario

<ol>
<li>A patient has a chronic condition with excess red blood cells and elevated hematocrit and hemoglobin. The physician identifies a medical need for bloodletting.</li>
<li>The physician prescribes drawing 350 ml per session, twice a week for two weeks, up to four sessions. Feedback is required after each session to monitor tolerance and response.</li>
</ol>

**Source discrepancy:** The final narrative step mentions five sessions every three days. This test follows the structured template: twice a week for two weeks, up to four sessions.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.html).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
