### Scenario

<ol>
<li>A patient with type 1 diabetes is treated by a multidisciplinary team at a recognised diabetes convention centre. After initial education and stabilisation, the team identifies a need for continued support at home.</li>
<li>A physician from the centre prescribes diabetes self-care education, with a maximum of 30 sessions and no required feedback.</li>
</ol>

**Source discrepancy:** The narrative specifies three sessions a week. The template leaves the frequency number blank. This test follows the narrative and does not infer a treatment duration from the maximum count.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.html).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
