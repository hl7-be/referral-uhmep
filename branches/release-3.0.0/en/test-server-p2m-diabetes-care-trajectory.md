## P2M - Nursing - Diabetes education within care trajectory

### Scenario

1. A patient with type 2 diabetes treated with insulin is enrolled in a diabetes care path and has a complete global medical file. During follow-up, the physician recommends education on using a blood glucose meter.
1. The physician prescribes five individual education sessions per year, with a maximum of the first five sessions and no required feedback. The validity ends on 31 December of the year in which validity starts.

**Source discrepancy:** The narrative selects blood glucose meter education. The template lists additional education topics without distinguishing selected values from options. This test follows the narrative and does not add the other topics.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
