## P2M - Nursing - Diabetes education without care trajectory

### Scenario

1. A patient who is not enrolled in a diabetes care path has difficulty managing medication, blood glucose monitoring and daily diabetes routines.
1. The physician prescribes ten individual self-care education sessions of 30 minutes, once a month, with a maximum of ten sessions and no required feedback. The general remarks specify "10 sessions of 30 min".

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
