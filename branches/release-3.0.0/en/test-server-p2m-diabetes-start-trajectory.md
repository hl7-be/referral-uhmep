## P2M - Nursing - Diabetes education within start trajectory

### Scenario

1. A 55-year-old patient with early-stage type 2 diabetes needs education to support lifestyle changes and treatment adherence.
1. The physician prescribes individual education sessions provided by a nurse under the preliminary diabetes care path: four sessions per year, with a maximum of four sessions and no required feedback. The validity ends on 31 December of the year in which validity starts.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
