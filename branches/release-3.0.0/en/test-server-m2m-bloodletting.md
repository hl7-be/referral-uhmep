## M2M - Nursing - Bloodletting

### Scenario

1. A patient has a chronic condition with excess red blood cells and elevated hematocrit and hemoglobin. The physician identifies a medical need for bloodletting.
1. The physician prescribes drawing 350 ml per session, twice a week for two weeks, up to four sessions. Feedback is required after each session to monitor tolerance and response.

For M2M, the organization submits creation on behalf of its internal professional.

**Source discrepancy:** The final narrative step mentions five sessions every three days. This test follows the structured template: twice a week for two weeks, up to four sessions.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
