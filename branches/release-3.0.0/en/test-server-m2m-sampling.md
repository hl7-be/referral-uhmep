## M2M - Nursing - Sampling

### Scenario

1. A patient presents with redness, swelling and pain in the right knee, fever and fatigue. The physician suspects a wound infection and requests diagnostic sampling.
1. The physician prescribes wound-fluid sampling from the right knee once a day for one week, up to seven sessions, without required feedback. The remarks specify wound fluid for bacterial culture.

For M2M, the organization submits creation on behalf of its internal professional.

**Source discrepancy:** The narrative also mentions a blood draw. The structured template specifies wound fluid only, which is the sample type used in this test.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
