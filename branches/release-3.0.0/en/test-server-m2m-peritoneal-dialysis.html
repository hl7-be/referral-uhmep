M2M - Nursing - Chronic peritoneal dialysis

  • Scenario
  • Create Prescription

Scenario

  1. A patient with kidney failure and loss of blood pressure is treated at a convention centre and cannot change dialysis bags independently.
  2. The centre's physician prescribes home nursing support three times a day for one year, up to 1095 sessions, with feedback required. The remarks specify a dialysis-fluid dwell time of two hours. The medical reason notes that support will only be needed as required once the patient learns to change the bags independently.

For M2M, the organization submits creation on behalf of its internal professional.

Create Prescription

POST /ServiceRequest

Profile: ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
