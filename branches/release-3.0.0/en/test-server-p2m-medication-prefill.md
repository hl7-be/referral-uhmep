## P2M - Nursing - Medication prefill preparation, not refunded

### Scenario

1. A patient reports difficulty adhering to her medication regimen. Following assessment, the physician finds no medical justification for reimbursed medication preparation, but the patient still requests assistance.
1. The physician creates a prescription for non-refundable medication prefill preparation once a week, without required feedback. The validity end date is no later than one year after the validity start date.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
