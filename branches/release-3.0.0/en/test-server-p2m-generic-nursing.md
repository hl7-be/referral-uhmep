## P2M - Nursing - Generic nursing care

### Scenario

1. A patient receiving parenteral nutrition consults her physician because of discomfort and difficulties maintaining adequate nutritional intake.
1. The physician prescribes generic nursing care for gastrointestinal concerns, with the diagnosis "Nutritional problems with tube feeding or parenteral nutrition": once a day for one week, up to seven sessions, without required feedback.

### Create Prescription

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-nursing](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md).

Retain the returned ServiceRequest id. Clinical plaintext in the table is represented by encrypted values in the request.
