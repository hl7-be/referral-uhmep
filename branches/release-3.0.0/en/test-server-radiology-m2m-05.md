## M2M - Radiology - Tongue weakness

### Scenario

1. A 25-year-old man presents with recent tongue weakness. The physician assesses associated speech, swallowing and neurological symptoms.
1. The physician selects brain MRI without and with contrast to investigate the neurological presentation.
1. After completing safety screening, the physician creates the MRI prescription. No neck CT is substituted for the examination described in the business narrative.

Business Data is authoritative: brain MRI without and with contrast is retained. Technical Data selects neck CT with contrast (431326009), which is not used. The MRI is coded using the catalogue head MRI with contrast procedure (432874000), with both unenhanced and enhanced phases retained in the encrypted examination description.

### Create Prescription: Brain MRI without and with contrast

`POST /ServiceRequest`

Profile: [ereferral-servicerequest-diagnosticimaging](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-diagnosticimaging.md).

The physician completes imaging safety screening and creates the prescription. Age and sex at birth remain visible as business values, while the contained patient carries encrypted values. Safety answers not supplied by the scenario are runtime inputs, not assumed negative findings. Complete and review them before submitting the completed questionnaire. The representing organization is identified in the requester reference.
