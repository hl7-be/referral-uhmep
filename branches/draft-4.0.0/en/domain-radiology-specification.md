## Radiology Specification

### Scope

Radiology prescriptions request diagnostic imaging and carry the procedure, indication, clinical context and supporting information required by the [Diagnostic Imaging ServiceRequest profile](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-diagnosticimaging.md).

### Prescription Types

The business fields, terminology and FHIR representation of diagnostic imaging prescriptions are detailed in [Radiology Mapping](mappings-radiology.md).

Radiology Test Server scenarios and their P2M/M2M JSON examples have not yet been specified. Existing radiology artifacts are documented under [Artifacts](artifacts.md) and are not implicitly designated as Test Server cases.
