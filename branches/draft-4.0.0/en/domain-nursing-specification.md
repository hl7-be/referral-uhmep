## Nursing Specification

### Scope

Nursing prescriptions describe requested nursing care and its clinical context. The principal resource is the [Nursing ServiceRequest profile](StructureDefinition-ereferral-servicerequest-nursing.md). Medication preparation under Annex 81 uses the separate [Annex 81 profile](StructureDefinition-ereferral-annex-81.md) and proposal workflow.

### Prescription Types

The supported nursing prescription types and their business fields are detailed in [Nursing Mapping](mappings-nursing.md).

The [Test Server scenarios](test-server.md#nursing) list the P2M cases currently provided for integrator testing. M2M cases are not specified yet.
