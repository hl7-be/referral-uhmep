Logical Model: BeModelReferralPrescription

Official URL: https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/drp/StructureDefinition/BeModelReferralPrescription Version: 1.1.1
Active as of 2026-10-28 Computable Name: BeModelReferralPrescription

Data model for the prescription referral.

Usages:

  • Derived from this Logical Model: BeModelNursingPrescription
  • Refer to this Logical Model: BeModelOrganisationClaim and BeModelTreatmentStatus

NameFlagsCard.TypeDescription & Constraints    Filter:



BeModelReferralPrescription 0..* Base BeModelReferralPrescription
Instances of this type can be the target of a Reference
BeModelReferralPrescription.identifier 1..1 Identifier Business identifier of the request
BeModelReferralPrescription.shortCode 1..1 CodeableConcept Business code allowing the healthcare professional to find a prescription associated with a patient
BeModelReferralPrescription.recordedDate 1..1 dateTime Date of recording of the information by the Recorder.
BeModelReferralPrescription.creationDate 1..1 dateTime Date of creation of the referral prescription
BeModelReferralPrescription.patient 1..1 Identifier Identification of the patient. The unique identifier must be: NISS, National Registry Number or Bis Patient
BeModelReferralPrescription.author 1..1 Identifier The person who encodes the prescription (e.g. a doctor, nurse, midwife or dentist) and takes responsibility for their content. The unique identifier must be: NISS, National Registry Number or Bis Requester
BeModelReferralPrescription.status 1..1 CodeableConcept Is the status of the referral prescription (e.g. planned, complete, stopped, suspended, in progress, ….)
BeModelReferralPrescription.statusReason 0..1 CodeableConcept Gives the reason for the status of the referral prescription (treatment has been changed, patient is allergic, patient refuses, …)
BeModelReferralPrescription.careRequested 1..1 CodeableConcept Type of care requested (e.g. 'Compression therapy', 'Sample collection', CT Scan).
BeModelReferralPrescription.discipline 1..1 CodeableConcept Category of the requested care (nursing, radiology, ...)
BeModelReferralPrescription.description 0..1 CodeableConcept Additional details on the requested care
BeModelReferralPrescription.type 1..1 CodeableConcept Type of the request or prescription ('Prescription' or 'Proposal').
BeModelReferralPrescription.originRequestId 0..1 Identifier Reference to the ID of the original request (useful when the request is an extension of another one).
BeModelReferralPrescription.validationStartDate 1..1 dateTime Start date of the prescription validity (encoded by the prescriber).
BeModelReferralPrescription.validationEndDate 1..1 dateTime End date of the prescription validity (encoded by the prescriber)
BeModelReferralPrescription.problem 0..* CodeableConcept Medical problem or clinical context related to this request (for example: 'Open wound', 'Broken leg').
BeModelReferralPrescription.bodyLocation 0..* CodeableConcept Anatomical location where the treatment should be applied (for example: 'Left arm').
